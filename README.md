# The AI Agent Market — 2026 Briefing

A single-page static briefing on the AI agent / agentic AI market as of September 2026.

**Live site:** https://isheetasnh.github.io/ai-agent-market/

## What's in it

- Headline market sizing for 2025–2026 and long-range forecasts
- Side-by-side comparison of five analyst houses (Grand View, Precedence, Mordor, Research and Markets, Gartner) and why their numbers disagree
- Enterprise adoption and deployment statistics
- Regional deployment shares
- Drivers vs. headwinds, and how the market is structured
- Links back to every source

## Caveat

All figures are third-party analyst estimates and survey results, reproduced as published and
not independently verified. Market-sizing methodologies differ materially between houses, so
cross-source comparisons are directional only.
